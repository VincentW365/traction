# 17_Discovery_Workshops_Question_Bank.md

## Purpose

Provide a practical workshop and interview guide for confirming requirements with the service centre team.

This is the fastest way to separate:
- what people say they want
- what the business actually needs
- what phase 1 must include

---

## 1. Workshop Sequence

### Workshop 1 — Current-State Walkthrough
Participants:
- service centre manager
- front desk / cashier
- service advisor
- at least one technician
- parts staff

Goal:
- map today's real process
- identify delays, manual workarounds, and disagreements

### Workshop 2 — Future-State Queue and Scheduling Policy
Participants:
- manager
- front desk
- advisor
- operations lead

Goal:
- resolve appointment vs walk-in priority
- resolve early/late/no-show rules
- define fairness and override policy

### Workshop 3 — Workshop and Parts Flow
Participants:
- advisor
- technicians
- parts staff
- manager

Goal:
- define how work scope becomes service order
- define parts readiness and blocking behavior
- define technician assignment rules

### Workshop 4 — Transport and Customer Communication
Participants:
- manager
- front desk
- transport coordinator / driver if applicable

Goal:
- define reminder cadence
- define transport eligibility
- define customer communication expectations

### Workshop 5 — Integrations and Reporting
Participants:
- manager
- finance / POS stakeholder
- inventory stakeholder
- reporting stakeholder if any

Goal:
- define external systems, constraints, and KPI expectations

## 2. Question Bank by Stakeholder

### Service Centre Manager
- What causes the biggest customer complaints today?
- Where does the current queue become confusing?
- Which policy decisions must the system enforce automatically?
- Where do you want manager override to exist?
- What would make you trust the queue logic?
- What KPIs do you need daily, weekly, monthly?

### Front Desk / Cashier
- What slows down check-in today?
- How do you currently handle early arrivals?
- How do you currently handle late appointments?
- How do you explain queue order to customers today?
- What fields do you truly need at check-in?
- Where do you lose time finding existing customers or vehicles?

### Service Advisor
- How do you decide service duration today?
- When does promised time go wrong?
- How do you decide who gets which job?
- What information do you need before assigning work?
- How do you handle scope changes after inspection?
- What should block a job from being promised or started?

### Technician
- What information do you need before starting a job?
- What statuses are important during work?
- How do parts delays interrupt you today?
- How often are you double-booked or interrupted?
- What would make a tablet/job queue useful instead of annoying?

### Parts / Store Staff
- When do you learn which parts are needed?
- How often do next-day jobs fail because parts were not staged?
- How do transfers work today?
- What are the most common shortage scenarios?
- Which substitutions are acceptable without manager involvement?

### Transport Coordinator / Driver
- What information is needed before dispatch?
- How far or long can a courtesy transport realistically run?
- What time windows matter most?
- What causes missed pickups?
- Do you need vehicle-only movements tracked separately from customer movements?

### Finance / POS Stakeholder
- What must sync into or out of the POS?
- When is payment considered final?
- Are there on-account customers or fleet accounts?
- What invoice states matter operationally?
- How can duplicates or mismatches happen today?

## 3. Artifact Outputs Required from Workshops

Every workshop should produce:
- decisions made
- rules confirmed
- open questions
- policy owner
- system impact
- phase assignment

Do not leave workshops with vague notes only.

---

## 4. Recommended Facilitation Structure

For each topic:
1. describe current process
2. show pain point
3. propose future-state rule
4. confirm exception handling
5. decide whether phase 1 or later
6. log open questions

---

## 5. Golden Questions That Prevent Scope Creep

- Is this a real operational need or a preference?
- Does this belong in phase 1?
- What happens when the happy path fails?
- What data is needed to report on this later?
- Who owns this decision in the business?
- What should the system do automatically versus merely highlight?

---

## 6. Workshop Warning Signs

Watch for:
- “we'll know it when we see it”
- conflicting queue fairness opinions
- no agreement on no-show rules
- no clear owner for technician assignment
- parts process described as informal memory
- transport described without capacity limits
- requests for advanced AI before base data is clean
